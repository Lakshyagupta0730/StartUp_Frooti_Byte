Company Overview

About FrootiBytes

FrootiBytes is an India-focused FMCG brand offering premium dehydrated fruit snacks as a joyful, nourishing alternative to conventional snacks. Its fruit-led products are made for households, snackers, professionals, parents, and curious consumers seeking better-for-you choices at home, at work, on the go, and beyond.

🎯 Our Mission

FrootiBytes exists to make everyday snacking more joyful, nourishing, and accessible by turning real fruit into a premium crunch people can choose over compromise.

🏗️ What We're Building

An accessible, better-for-you alternative to potato chips. We bring fruit-led flavor through Kiranas, modern trade, DTC, e-commerce, and export partners, supported by digital lot traceability.

🚀 Where We're Headed

A world where the snack bowl is no longer a choice between bland virtue and indulgent regret. Connecting farmers, makers, retailers, and families through a visible, accountable food chain.

Who We Serve

Households, snackers, professionals, parents, and curious global consumers seeking better-for-you fruit-based snacks, plus Kirana and other retail buyers seeking accessible-premium fruit snack products.

Brand Voice

Bright, joyful, nourishing, accessible, and trustworthy.
